# Activity Log / Company Timesheet

Master work-breakdown log. One row per task per day. Append new rows under the
current month's section. Status = `Done`, `In progress`, or `Blocked`.
Hours are actual time spent, to the nearest 0.25 (e.g. `2.5`) — never padded to 8h.

> Last submitted timesheet: **(set `lastSubmitted` in `~\.claude\timesheet-config.json`)**
> Ask Claude to "backfill since <date>" to reconstruct unlogged days.
